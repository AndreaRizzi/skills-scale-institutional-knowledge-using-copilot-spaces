# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads ensure product quality by defining testing strategies, executing test plans, and validating acceptance criteria. They work closely with product and development teams to prevent defects and ensure reliable delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Define testing strategy (unit, integration, end-to-end, security) aligned with project scope
- Create and maintain test plans and acceptance criteria validation checklists
- Execute manual and automated testing; report defects with clear reproduction steps
- Participate in release readiness assessments and smoke testing
- Collaborate with developers on test coverage and CI/CD integration
- Identify and escalate quality risks

### Goals
- Ensure features meet acceptance criteria before release
- Reduce production defects and improve reliability
- Enable fast, confident deployments through robust testing

### Typical Communication
- Test plan reviews and design walkthroughs with developers
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- QA sign-off on release checklists

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Collaborate on test coverage, automation strategies, and defect resolution
- **Product Managers**: Validate acceptance criteria and prioritize quality risks
- **Project Managers**: Report quality status and release readiness assessments

---

## Security/Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
Security/Compliance Officers protect the organization by embedding security practices into project workflows, managing security reviews, and ensuring compliance with regulations and standards.

### Responsibilities
- Define security requirements and threat models for projects
- Conduct or coordinate security reviews and code audits
- Configure and monitor security scanning in CI/CD pipelines
- Advise on compliance with data protection, access control, and incident response standards
- Manage security incident response and post-incident reviews
- Participate in risk assessments and dependency reviews

### Goals
- Prevent security vulnerabilities and data breaches
- Maintain compliance with regulatory and organizational standards
- Enable teams to build security into every release

### Typical Communication
- Security design reviews and threat modeling sessions
- Pre-release security checklists and sign-offs
- Incident response coordination and post-incident retrospectives

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Advise on secure coding practices and review PRs for security compliance
- **Project Managers**: Flag security risks and escalate compliance issues
- **QA/Testing Leads**: Coordinate security testing and vulnerability validation

---

## Stakeholder/Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors represent business needs, approve prioritization, and ensure projects align with organizational strategy. They provide governance, funding decisions, and executive visibility.

### Responsibilities
- Define business objectives and constraints for the project
- Approve project charter, scope, and budget
- Provide feedback on trade-offs and prioritization
- Communicate project outcomes to senior leadership
- Escalate blockers that require executive decision-making
- Participate in key gates (initiation, planning, release, retrospectives)

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver measurable business value
- Align technical delivery with strategic priorities
- Reduce delivery risk through early decision-making

### Typical Communication
- Project initiation and planning reviews
- Monthly or milestone-based status updates
- Release announcements and impact measurement

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Product Managers**: Provide business context and approve strategic prioritization
- **Project Managers**: Receive status updates and make executive decisions on scope/budget
- **Developers & QA**: Review outcomes and provide feedback on delivery quality

---

## Agile Coach/Scrum Master

### Role Summary
Agile Coaches and Scrum Masters foster team collaboration, remove process impediments, and facilitate continuous improvement. They ensure the team adheres to agreed workflows and holds retrospectives.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint ceremonies (planning, standups, reviews, retrospectives)
- Identify and help resolve impediments and blockers
- Coach team on Agile practices and process improvements
- Track velocity and team health metrics
- Manage retrospective action items and follow-up
- Support adoption of new tools and practices

### Goals
- Maximize team productivity and morale
- Enable predictable, iterative delivery
- Foster a culture of continuous improvement

### Typical Communication
- Facilitation of all sprint ceremonies
- One-on-ones with team members on challenges
- Tracking and reporting on process health

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **All Team Members**: Facilitate ceremonies, coach on practices, and support process improvements
- **Project Managers**: Collaborate on planning and remove organizational blockers
- **Developers**: Help identify technical impediments and support estimation

---

## Technical Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Architects provide technical leadership, design guidance, and risk assessment to ensure solutions are scalable, maintainable, and aligned with organizational standards.

### Responsibilities
- Participate in design reviews and provide architectural guidance
- Assess technical feasibility and identify architectural risks
- Ensure solutions align with organizational standards and best practices
- Advise on technology choices and trade-offs (performance, security, maintainability)
- Contribute to technical documentation and design decisions
- Review code and PRs for architectural compliance

### Goals
- Ensure technical solutions are robust, scalable, and maintainable
- Reduce technical debt and long-term support burden
- Enable consistency across projects and teams

### Typical Communication
- Architecture design reviews and RFCs
- Code reviews and PR feedback
- Technical planning and estimation discussions

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Provide design guidance, review PRs, and mentor on architectural patterns
- **Product Managers**: Advise on technical feasibility and trade-offs
- **Security/Compliance Officer**: Ensure architectural alignment with security and compliance standards
